# SPATIAL AR

A webcam-powered AR mini-game. Capture, stretch, rotate, and compress an energy core with hand gestures. Built with MediaPipe and Canvas.

通过摄像头识别双手，用抓取、拉伸、旋转和压缩四个动作操控能量核心。

## Screenshots / 界面预览

Actual screenshots from the current app. Gameplay screenshots use the built-in **simulation mode**, with synthetic hand landmarks and no camera feed.

以下为当前页面实截；游戏过程使用内置**模拟模式**，显示合成手部骨骼，不包含真人摄像头画面。

### Home / 首页

Start the webcam experience or try the camera-free simulation.

开启摄像头体验，或选择无需摄像头的模拟模式。

![Spatial AR homepage](docs/01-home.png)

### Capture / 抓取核心

Bring your hand near the core and pinch to capture it. Keep holding for about 2.5 seconds.

靠近核心并捏合，保持约 2.5 秒完成抓取。

![Capture stage in simulation mode](docs/02-capture.png)

### Stretch & Rotate / 拉伸与旋转

Move your hands apart to expand the core, then rotate your hands or wrists for at least 3 seconds. The screenshot shows the expanded core at the rotation stage.

双手拉开使核心展开，再转动双手或手腕至少 3 秒。图中展示展开后进入旋转阶段的核心。

![Expanded core at the rotation stage](docs/03-rotate.png)

### Complete / 完成挑战

Bring your palms closer to compress and release the core. Continue to the next round or exit.

双掌靠近，压缩核心并自动释放能量。完成后可继续挑战或退出。

![Completed challenge](docs/04-complete.png)

## About / 关于

Minimalist monochrome visuals, progressive challenge rounds, and local camera processing. No camera video is uploaded.

极简黑白视觉、多轮渐进挑战，摄像头画面仅在本机处理，不上传。
